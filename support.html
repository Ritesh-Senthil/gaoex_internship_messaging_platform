Common questions

How do I install the app?

GAOEX Connect is distributed as an unlisted App Store app. Use the download link shared by your program facilitator (it will not appear in App Store search).

How do I sign in?

Open the app and tap Continue with Google. Use the Google account invited to your internship program.

How do I join a program?

Ask your facilitator for an invite code, then use Join Program in the app and enter the code.

I’m not getting notifications

Check iOS Settings → GAOEX Connect → Notifications, and confirm the channel or conversation isn’t muted in the app (bell icon in the chat header).

Privacy & data

Read our Privacy Policy. To request account or data deletion, email support with the subject “Data deletion request”.

Report a problem

Include your name, the Google email you use to sign in, your device model / iOS version, and a short description (screenshots help). Email: ritesh.senthilr@gmail.com
