Why It Exists
Interns deserve better tools.

Existing platforms aren't built for internship programs. InternHub is.

😓

The Problem

  • Scattered communication across email, Slack, GroupMe, WhatsApp
  • No structured organization for different programs
  • No role-based access control for facilitators vs. interns
  • Hard to find shared resources and pinned information
  • Generic tools not designed for the internship experience
🚀

The Solution

  • One app for everything — messaging, files, announcements
  • Programs with organized channels and categories
  • Facilitators and interns have tailored permissions
  • Pinned messages, search, and shared resources built in
  • Purpose-built for how internship programs actually work

Organization
Structured for how programs run.

Each internship program is its own space — with channels, roles, and members that make sense.

🏛️

Programs

Create separate spaces for each internship cohort, department, or initiative. Each program has its own channels, members, and settings. Invite participants with a simple code.

📂

Channels & Categories

Organize conversations into channels grouped by category — Welcome, General, Resources, or whatever fits your program. Text channels for discussion, announcement channels for updates.

✉️

Direct & Group Messages

Private 1-on-1 conversations or group chats with up to 8 people. Perfect for mentor check-ins, project teams, or quick questions — with all the same features as channels.

🔐

Private Channels

Restrict access to specific channels for leadership discussions, sensitive topics, or team-specific spaces. Control exactly who sees what.
