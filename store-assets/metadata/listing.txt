Description (4000 chars max)
------------------------------------------------
GAOEX Connect is a mobile-first team collaboration app built for internship programs. Channels, direct messages, roles, and file sharing — designed for facilitators and interns.

PROGRAMS
Create or join program spaces with invite codes. Each program has its own channels, roles, and members — ideal for cohorts and teams.

REAL-TIME MESSAGING
Send messages instantly in channels and DMs. Support for formatting, @mentions, emoji reactions, and threaded replies.

CHANNELS & CATEGORIES
Organize conversations with text and announcement channels, grouped into categories. Pin important messages for quick access.

DIRECT MESSAGES & GROUPS
Chat 1-on-1 or in small groups. See typing indicators and presence in real time.

FILE SHARING
Share images, documents, videos, and other files directly in any conversation.

ROLES & PERMISSIONS
A flexible role system with granular permissions so facilitators control who can manage channels, invite members, and more.

SEARCH
Find messages, channels, and people across your workspace.

PUSH NOTIFICATIONS
Get notified for new messages, @mentions, and DMs — even when the app is in the background.

Sign in securely with Google. Built for internship communities that need a focused place to collaborate.
